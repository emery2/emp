01 The Problem
Most organizations run on ten tools, not one: an HR system, a CRM, spreadsheets for
projects, an inbox for support, and chat holding it together. Nothing talks to anything else,
and every department sees a different, incomplete picture of the business.

Rigid software

The cost shows up as slow approvals, duplicated data entry, leadership without a cross-department view,
and IT teams maintaining a stack of tools never designed to work together.

02 The Solution
EMP is a platform of separate services. You choose the ones you need, such as HR,
CRM, partners, performance, projects, or service desk, and switch on more when the
business is ready. Every service works on its own. When you use several together, they
share the same people, the same organization structure, and the same permissions, so
information is entered once and used everywhere.

Example: an employee requests leave
Leave follows the usual two-part rule: the person who knows the workload decides, and HR checks the
policy.
STEP WHO WHAT HAPPENS
1 Employee Submits a leave request with dates and leave type.
2 Head of
Department
Approves or rejects based on workload and team cover.
3 HR Verifies balance and policy, then records the leave.
4 System Updates the balance, team calendar, and dashboards, and notifies everyone
involved. Task assignment views show the employee as away.
Statutory leave such as sick or maternity leave skips the line decision: HR verifies and records it, and the
Head of Department is informed. Nobody can approve their own request. Each organization can change
these rules without code changes.

04 The Services
Services are grouped by what they help you do. Each can be adopted alone or combined.
People & Work
HR: employee records, recruitment, attendance, leave, training. Projects & Tasks: departments assign and
track work. EPM (performance): objectives, KPIs, scorecards, and review cycles that connect company
goals to individual work.
Customers & Partners
CRM: leads, customers, and a pipeline you configure. Service Desk: internal and external tickets with SLAs.
PRM: vendors, suppliers, resellers, agreements, and a partner portal. CX: surveys and a complete customer
view.
Operations
Sales, Procurement, Inventory, Logistics, Planning, Manufacturing, and Finance cover the order-to-
cash and purchase-to-pay cycles, stock, production, and accounting. These services are on the roadmap
and are added by client demand.

08 Getting Started
Choose your services. Start with one or two, or pick a bundle.
Register the organization. An administrator provides the name, subdomain, and admin email. Setup
of the workspace, default roles, and storage runs automatically.
Build your structure. Create departments and appoint a Head of Department for each.
Invite your team. Assign roles; each person sees only what their role allows.
Configure the rules. Leave policy, sales pipeline stages, approval chains, and branding are settings,
not projects.
Add services as you grow. Switching one on keeps your existing data and connects it automatically.
The services you need. The structure you already have.
EMP grows with the organization, from a single service to the full suite.
